## What the benefit is

The Canada child benefit is a federal payment to an eligible parent. It is not taxable. It is not a deduction on the return. The estimate on this page uses the federal amounts only. Provincial and territorial child benefits are not added.

The rate file’s period is the benefit year it names. For a child under six the annual amount in the file is {money:house.ccb.under6}. For a child from six to seventeen it is {money:house.ccb.six}. The phase-out starts from the income thresholds in that same file, including {money:house.ccb.first} and {money:house.ccb.second}.

## How the calculation works

The base is the sum of the age amounts for the children you enter. Adjusted family net income above the first threshold reduces the payment. A higher income, past {money:house.ccb.second}, uses the steeper reduction. Shared custody, when you turn it on, uses half the benefit. The form does not assume shared custody.

## Worked examples

One child under six and income below the first threshold produces the base of {money:house.ccb.under6}, before any reduction. Income above {money:house.ccb.first} reduces that base. The reduction is not a flat fee.

Two children, or a mix of ages, add the second child’s amount. A child aged six to seventeen adds {money:house.ccb.six}, not another {money:house.ccb.under6}. Shared custody then halves the result you would have had as the only caregiver.

## Common mistakes

Adding a provincial benefit that this page never calculated. Using the under-six amount for a teenager. Leaving shared custody off when the child lives with the other parent half the time, or turning it on when you have full custody.

## Recent changes

The amounts {money:house.ccb.under6} and {money:house.ccb.six}, and the thresholds, are the figures in the file checked {updated}. Benefits are indexed. A July payment cycle can use a new income figure from the return you just filed. This page does not restate a payment CRA has already deposited.

## Questions

### Is the benefit taxable?

No. Do not add it to the income you type, and do not expect a tax deduction for receiving it.

### Are provincial benefits included?

No. Apply for those separately. This estimate is the federal benefit only.

### What does shared custody do?

It uses half the benefit. It is a switch you set. The form does not infer your parenting arrangement.

### Why does family income matter?

The payment is reduced as adjusted family net income rises past {money:house.ccb.first}, and the reduction changes again past {money:house.ccb.second}.

### Does the age of the child matter?

Yes. Under six uses {money:house.ccb.under6}. From six to seventeen uses {money:house.ccb.six}.
