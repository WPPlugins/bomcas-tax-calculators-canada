## What is being compared

A lease and a purchase spread the cost of a vehicle differently. A purchase borrows the price, and you own the car at the end if the loan is paid. A lease pays for the use of the car, and you may pay a buyout to keep it. This page discounts both streams at the rate you type. It is not a dealer’s worksheet and it does not know a manufacturer incentive you forgot to enter.

## How the calculation works

The purchase side uses the price, the down payment, the interest rate, and the term, then subtracts the resale you expect. Sales tax on the purchase follows the general rate when the form applies it. The lease side uses the payment you were offered, the term, and the buyout, and can apply sales tax to each payment when you say so. The discount rate pulls future payments back to today’s money so a low monthly lease is not treated as cheaper just because the cheque is smaller.

## Worked examples

A lease with a low payment and a high buyout can cost more, once the buyout is included, than a loan on the same vehicle. Comparing the monthly lease payment with the monthly loan payment, and ignoring the buyout and the resale, picks the wrong option.

A purchase with a high down payment has a smaller loan but ties up cash now. The discount rate is how the form weighs that cash against later payments. Changing the discount rate changes which option looks cheaper. It does not change the contract.

Sales tax on each lease payment is not the same as sales tax on the full purchase price. Turn the tax switch on only when the offer taxes the payment. British Columbia PST and GST are not assumed if you leave the switch off.

## Common mistakes

Forgetting the lease buyout. Using a hopeful resale. Comparing monthly payments of different lengths. Treating the result as the dealer’s out-the-door price.

## Questions

### Why not just compare monthly payments?

Because the lease may end with a buyout, and the purchase may end with a car you can sell. Those amounts are part of the cost.

### Does the page know my province’s vehicle tax?

It applies the general sales-tax treatment you select. A vehicle-specific rule belongs on the vehicle page. Do not assume this screen replaced that rule.

### What is the discount rate?

The rate used to compare money paid in different years. It is not the loan rate unless you type the same number for both.

### Is a cheaper lease always better?

No. A cheap payment with a large buyout can be the more expensive contract after the buyout is counted.

### Are incentives included?

Only if you reduce the price or the payment you type. The form does not look up a rebate.
