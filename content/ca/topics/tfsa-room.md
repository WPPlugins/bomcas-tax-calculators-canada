## What TFSA room is

A tax-free savings account lets eligible residents contribute up to their room. Growth and qualifying withdrawals are not taxed. The contribution is not deducted from income. That is the difference from an RRSP, where a contribution within room is deductible, and from an FHSA, which is also deductible and aimed at a first home.

The {year_label} annual limit in the rate file is {money:house.tfsa.limit}.

## How the calculation works

Start with the annual limit. Add room you carried forward. Add withdrawals from the previous year, which come back as room in the following year, not the same year. Subtract contributions already made this year. The form does not know your CRA balance. If you omit a contribution, the room is too high.

## Worked examples

A person who has never contributed and is eligible for the current year only has {money:house.tfsa.limit} of new room from this year’s limit. Earlier years are included only when you add them. The form will not reconstruct every year since the TFSA began unless you enter those limits.

Someone who withdrew an amount last year adds that withdrawal to this year’s room, on top of {money:house.tfsa.limit} and any other carry-forward. A withdrawal made this year does not restore room until next year.

Someone who also contributes {money:house.fhsa.annual} to an FHSA has not used TFSA room. The two accounts are separate. Contributing {money:house.rrsp.limit} to an RRSP does not use TFSA room either.

## Common mistakes

Subtracting a withdrawal in the same year it was made. Treating the contribution as a deduction. Adding the annual limit twice because a January contribution was also counted in the previous year.

## Recent changes

The annual limit of {money:house.tfsa.limit} is the {year_label} figure checked {updated}. Limits have changed from year to year. Use the year you mean. Do not apply this year’s limit to a past year.

## Questions

### Is a TFSA contribution deductible?

No. The tax benefit is that qualifying growth and withdrawals are not included in income.

### Does my province change the room?

No. {money:house.tfsa.limit} is the federal annual limit.

### When does a withdrawal come back as room?

In the year after the withdrawal, not in the year you took the money out.

### Can I use RRSP room here?

No. RRSP room is {rate:house.rrsp} of earned income, up to {money:house.rrsp.limit}. It is a different plan.

### Does the form read my CRA account?

No. You enter the room and the contributions. What you type stays in the browser.
