## What the levy is

British Columbia, Ontario, Manitoba, and Newfoundland and Labrador levy a payroll tax on employers. The names differ. The file stores each province’s method, exemption, and rate by year. Other provinces are not given a silent zero. If you pick a province the file does not tax, the screen should say the levy is not estimated, not that the tax is zero.

## How the calculation works

Choose the province and the year. The exemption, where the method has one, comes off the payroll before the rate. A notch method charges a higher rate through a band above the exemption and then a lower rate on the whole payroll once the band is passed. Ontario’s method phases the exemption out as payroll grows and then applies the rate band tied to total payroll. The form reads those rules from the year you picked.

## Worked examples

Payroll at or under the exemption owes nothing under an exemption method. A dollar above the exemption is not taxed as if the whole payroll lost the exemption, unless the method you selected works that way. Read the year’s method. Do not assume every province notches the same way.

A notch province can owe more inside the notch band than it owes once payroll is high enough to use the full rate on the whole payroll. That result surprises people. It is the notch, not a bug, when the file’s method is a notch.

Manitoba’s thresholds are not British Columbia’s. Using one province’s exemption for the other misstates the levy. The year matters as well. A threshold from the prior year is the wrong input when you selected the current year.

## Common mistakes

Calling this personal income tax. An employee’s Ontario Health Premium is not this employer levy. Mixing remittance dates for income-tax withholding with the instalments this levy requires. Estimating a province the file does not cover and reading a blank as zero.

## Recent changes

Each year in the file has its own exemption and rates, checked {updated}. When a budget moves a threshold, the new year in the file is the one to select. The screen does not blend two years.

## Questions

### Which provinces are covered?

The ones in the employer-health file: British Columbia, Ontario, Manitoba, and Newfoundland and Labrador. A province that is absent is not taxed at zero by this page.

### Is this the Ontario Health Premium on my T1?

No. That premium is personal. This levy is on the employer’s payroll.

### Why would a higher payroll owe less?

In a notch method, the band above the exemption can be charged at a higher rate than the full rate that applies once payroll clears the band. The year’s row in the file is the authority.

### Do I use last year’s exemption?

Only if you selected last year. The year control chooses the row.

### Are instalments included?

The levy may require instalments. This screen estimates the annual tax from the payroll you type. It does not print a remittance calendar.
