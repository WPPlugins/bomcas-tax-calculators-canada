## What the premium is

When a down payment is under 20 percent, a high-ratio mortgage is generally insured. The premium depends on the loan-to-value band. This page uses the CMHC table in the rate file, including the Home Start table when you turn that on, and the non-traditional down-payment loading when that box applies. It does not shop among insurers and it does not say a lender must use CMHC.

## How the calculation works

Price minus down payment is the loan, before the premium. Loan divided by price is the loan-to-value ratio. The table assigns a premium rate to that band. The premium can be added to the mortgage. Adding it increases the amount you borrow and the interest you pay. Amortization past 25 years, where the table has a different column, is not the same premium as a 25-year loan.

A down payment of 20 percent or more is outside the insured bands this form prices. Do not force a premium onto a conventional mortgage.

## Worked examples

A small down payment lands in a higher band and a higher premium than a down payment just below 20 percent. Moving across a band boundary changes the rate on the whole premium calculation the table describes, not a tax bracket on income.

Home Start, when the purchase qualifies and you select it, uses the Home Start rates in the file rather than the standard grid. Leaving the box off prices the standard grid. Turning it on when the buyer does not qualify produces a premium you cannot rely on.

A non-traditional down payment, in the band where the file adds a loading, costs more than the same loan-to-value funded with a traditional down payment. The box is that fact. It is not a guess about where the money came from.

## Common mistakes

Insuring a mortgage that does not need insurance. Using the standard grid for a Home Start purchase. Ignoring the amortization column. Treating the premium as a one-time fee that does not affect interest when you add it to the principal.

## Questions

### Is insurance required at 20 percent down?

Not under the high-ratio rule this page uses. The premium table is for loans above that loan-to-value.

### Does the premium earn interest?

If you add it to the mortgage, you pay interest on it for the amortization. Paying it in cash avoids that interest.

### What is Home Start?

A premium table for qualifying buyers, stored separately in the file. Select it only when it applies.

### Can I use a private insurer’s rate here?

No. The table is the CMHC schedule in the file. Another insurer’s price is not estimated.

### Is this a loan approval?

No. It is the premium on the numbers you type.
