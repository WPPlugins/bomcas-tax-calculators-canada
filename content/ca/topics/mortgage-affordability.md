## What the ratios are

Lenders test housing costs against income. The gross-debt ratio in the file is {rate:house.mortgage.gds}. The total-debt ratio is {rate:house.mortgage.tds}. The gross-debt test looks at principal, interest, property tax, heat, and half of condo fees. The total-debt test adds other debt payments. This page applies those ratios. It does not approve a mortgage and it does not know a lender’s own exceptions.

## How the calculation works

The contract rate you type is not always the rate used to qualify. The stress test uses the greater of the contract rate plus the buffer in the file, and the floor rate in the file. A low contract rate does not remove the floor. Amortization and the down payment change the payment the ratios can support.

## Worked examples

A household with no other debt can put more of the {rate:house.mortgage.tds} room toward the mortgage, because the total-debt test is not already used by a car loan. The gross-debt test of {rate:house.mortgage.gds} can still be the one that binds.

A household with a large monthly debt payment can fail the total-debt test even when housing costs alone fit under {rate:house.mortgage.gds}. Paying off the other debt changes the result. Stretching the amortization to pass a test changes the interest you pay over the life of the loan. The form shows the payment. It does not tell you the loan is wise.

A down payment under 20 percent can require mortgage insurance. The premium is not a hidden discount. The CMHC page is where the premium table is estimated.

## Common mistakes

Qualifying at the contract rate when the stress test is higher. Forgetting heat and property tax. Treating condo fees as fully included when the test uses half. Reading the result as a bank’s commitment.

## Questions

### Is this a pre-approval?

No. It is the ratio test from the file, using the income and debts you type.

### Which ratio wins?

Whichever one fails first. Other debt pushes you into the {rate:house.mortgage.tds} test. Housing costs alone are the {rate:house.mortgage.gds} test.

### Does a low advertised rate qualify me?

Not by itself. The stress test can use a higher rate than the contract.

### Are condo fees fully counted?

The standard test counts half of condo fees in the housing costs. Enter the fee the form asks for. Do not double it.

### Where is the insurance premium?

On the CMHC page, when the down payment requires insurance. It is not buried in these ratios.
