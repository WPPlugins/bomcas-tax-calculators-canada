## What the tax is

Quebec municipalities collect a duty on the transfer of an immovable, often called the welcome tax. The duty uses brackets. For the year in the file, the first bracket ends at {money:house.welcome.first} and the second at {money:house.welcome.second}. Rates step up above those amounts. This is not British Columbia property transfer tax and it is not Ontario land transfer tax. Montreal can charge a higher duty above a further threshold. If you do not enter Montreal’s extra rule, the form uses the general brackets only.

## How the calculation works

The base is the consideration or the municipal value, whichever the rules use for that transfer. The base is split across the brackets. Only the slice inside a bracket is charged at that bracket’s rate. A year the file does not list is not estimated from the neighbouring year.

## Worked examples

A price inside the first bracket, at or under {money:house.welcome.first}, uses only the first rate. A price between {money:house.welcome.first} and {money:house.welcome.second} uses the first rate on the first slice and the second rate on the rest. A price above {money:house.welcome.second} uses the third rate on the excess. The whole price is not charged at the top rate.

A Montreal transfer can cost more than the general table once the city’s additional bracket applies. Leaving that out understates the duty. Turning it on for a transfer outside Montreal overstates it.

## Common mistakes

Using Ontario’s land transfer tax on a Quebec deed. Using one rate on the entire price. Ignoring the higher of price and municipal value when the statute uses that comparison. Quoting a {year_label} bracket for a deed in a year the file has replaced.

## Recent changes

The bracket ends of {money:house.welcome.first} and {money:house.welcome.second} are the amounts in the file for the year that token selects, checked {updated}. Quebec indexes them. A deed is taxed under the brackets in force for that deed, not under a rounded number from memory.

## Questions

### Is this the same as Ontario land transfer tax?

No. The brackets and the collector are different. Toronto’s municipal tax is not Montreal’s additional duty.

### Is the whole price taxed at the top rate?

No. Each slice uses its own rate. The top rate applies to the slice above {money:house.welcome.second}.

### What if the municipal assessment is higher than the price?

The duty’s base follows the rule for that transfer, which can be the higher amount. Type the base the deed and the assessment require. The form does not fetch the roll.

### Are there exemptions?

Some transfers are exempt. This estimate does not apply an exemption you did not establish. It prices the general brackets.

### Which year do I use?

The year of the transfer, if the file contains it. A missing year is not filled with the next year’s thresholds.
