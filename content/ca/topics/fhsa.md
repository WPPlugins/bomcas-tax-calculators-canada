## What an FHSA is

A first home savings account lets a qualifying first-time buyer contribute, deduct the contribution, and withdraw for a qualifying home without including that withdrawal in income. It is a federal plan. There is no separate FHSA page for each province. The room does not change because you live in British Columbia or Ontario.

For {year_label} the annual limit is {money:house.fhsa.annual}. The lifetime limit is {money:house.fhsa.lifetime}. Unused room carries forward, and that carry-forward is capped at one year's annual limit. The most you can contribute in one year is twice the annual limit, {money:house.fhsa.annual} from the new year plus {money:house.fhsa.annual} of unused room. You cannot save several unused years and then contribute the lifetime limit at once.

## How the calculation works

In the year you open the account, the room starts at the annual limit of {money:house.fhsa.annual}. In a later year the room is that new annual limit plus unused room carried forward, and the carry-forward cannot be more than one year's annual limit. The contribution in that year therefore cannot be more than twice {money:house.fhsa.annual}. Every dollar contributed also counts toward the lifetime limit of {money:house.fhsa.lifetime}.

The form asks for what you have already contributed and what room you are carrying. It does not know your notice of assessment. If those inputs are wrong, the remaining room is wrong.

A withdrawal for a qualifying home is not taxed. A withdrawal that is not for a qualifying home is included in income. This page does not decide whether a particular purchase qualifies.

## Worked examples

Suppose the account is new and nothing has been contributed. The room to contribute this year is the annual limit, {money:house.fhsa.annual}. That contribution is the amount you can deduct. It is not an extra {money:house.fhsa.annual} on top of the annual limit.

Suppose last year you opened the account and contributed nothing. Unused room of one annual limit carries forward. This year’s room is the new {money:house.fhsa.annual} plus that carry-forward, which is twice the annual limit. The lifetime counter still moves toward {money:house.fhsa.lifetime}. After the lifetime limit is reached, later annual room does not reopen it.

Suppose you want to contribute {money:house.fhsa.lifetime} in one year because that is the lifetime limit. The annual cap stops you. The lifetime limit is the ceiling over the life of the plan, not this year’s room.

## Programs and interactions

An FHSA contribution is not an RRSP contribution. The Home Buyers’ Plan is a different program: it is a withdrawal from an RRSP that has to be repaid. Do not enter an HBP repayment as an FHSA contribution.

There is no provincial FHSA room to add.

## Common mistakes

Adding {money:house.fhsa.annual} for every year you were an adult before the account existed. Room starts when you open the account, not from age 18 in the abstract. Treating {money:house.fhsa.lifetime} as this year’s limit. Withdrawing for a purchase that is not a qualifying home and expecting the withdrawal to stay tax-free.

## How this differs from RRSP and TFSA room

RRSP room is {rate:house.rrsp} of earned income, up to {money:house.rrsp.limit} for {year_label}, minus a pension adjustment. TFSA room for the year is {money:house.tfsa.limit}, and it is not deductible. FHSA room is a flat annual amount, it is deductible, and it is aimed at a first home. The three rooms are not added together into one savings limit.

## Questions

### How much can I put in during the first year?

{money:house.fhsa.annual}, if you open the account and you are allowed to contribute. That is the annual room, not the lifetime limit.

### Can unused room stack without a cap?

No. Unused room carries forward only up to one annual limit of {money:house.fhsa.annual}. With a full carry-forward, the most you can put in during a later year is twice that annual limit. You cannot bank the entire {money:house.fhsa.lifetime} and deposit it at once.

### Does my province add more room?

No. The limits are federal. Living in a particular province does not change {money:house.fhsa.annual}.

### Is a contribution deductible?

Yes, within the room and the other conditions of the plan. The deduction is not a credit calculated on this page’s provincial brackets.

### What if the withdrawal is not for a qualifying home?

It is included in income. The tax-free treatment is for a qualifying withdrawal.

### Is this the Home Buyers’ Plan?

No. The Home Buyers’ Plan borrows from an RRSP and is repaid. An FHSA withdrawal for a qualifying home is not that loan.
