## Who this is for

This estimate is for a person with self-employment income, not a paycheque. There is no employer to withhold tax or to pay half of the Canada Pension Plan. You pay both halves of CPP on pensionable self-employment earnings, and you pay income tax in instalments when the instalment test says so. The return for a person who carried on a business can be filed by {deadline:file_self_employed}. The balance is still due {deadline:balance_due}.

## How the calculation works

Self-employment income is taxed under the federal brackets and the provincial brackets for the year and province you select. British Columbia starts at {rate:BC.income.first} when that is the province. The basic personal amount still applies. CPP on self-employment is added. It is not the employee-only amount that appears on a T4. Voluntary EI is included only when you say you are paying it.

Quebec uses the Quebec Pension Plan instead of CPP for this income. Do not add CPP on top of QPP.

## Worked examples

A profit that is similar to a salary is not taxed like a salary. The salary page assumes an employer withheld tax and paid the employer half of CPP. Here both halves are yours, and nothing was withheld unless you paid instalments.

A person who also has employment income may already have contributed CPP on the job. This form’s CPP line should not ignore that if you can enter it. Double-counting the year’s maximum overstates the contribution.

A person under the instalment threshold still owes the balance on {deadline:balance_due}. The later filing date is not a later payment date.

## Common mistakes

Using the filing deadline as the payment deadline. Forgetting the employer half of CPP. Treating net self-employment income as if a T4 had already withheld tax. Mixing QPP and CPP.

## Questions

### Can I file in June and pay in June?

You may be able to file by {deadline:file_self_employed}. You pay a balance by {deadline:balance_due}.

### Why is CPP higher than on a paycheque?

Because you pay both the employee and employer shares on pensionable self-employment earnings. A paycheque only shows the employee share.

### Does this create RRSP room?

Earned income, including self-employment earnings, builds next year’s RRSP room at {rate:house.rrsp}, up to {money:house.rrsp.limit}. This page does not print that future room as a deduction against this year’s tax.

### Is Quebec CPP?

No. Quebec uses the Quebec Pension Plan. Do not stack CPP on QPP.

### Are business expenses already removed?

Only if the income you type is net of them. The form does not audit receipts.
