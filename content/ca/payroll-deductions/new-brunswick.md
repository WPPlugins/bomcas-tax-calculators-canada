## Who this is for

This estimate is one New Brunswick paycheque. The provincial line is New Brunswick income tax, not harmonized sales tax and not a city tax. The annual table starts at {rate:NB.income.first}.

Canada Pension Plan contributions use the employee rate of {rate:CPP.employee} up to pensionable earnings of {money:CPP.ympe}. Employment insurance uses {rate:EI.employee}. A claim code, a taxable benefit, and a bonus are not in the sample. The employer’s remitter type is not looked up.

## How the calculation works

Annual tax is divided by the number of pays. Federal and New Brunswick amounts are withheld separately. CPP and EI are employee contributions. Sales tax is not taken off the gross on this form.

## Common mistakes

Adding HST to the cheque because the province charges HST in shops. HST is not a payroll deduction. Using Nova Scotia’s income-tax basic amount on a New Brunswick payroll. Comparing the net with a Quebec net the sample does not calculate.
