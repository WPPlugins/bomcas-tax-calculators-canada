## What this tax is

Yukon does not add a territorial vehicle tax in this table. The dealer line is GST. A car taxed under British Columbia’s rules is a British Columbia invoice, even if the buyer lives in Yukon. Registration in Yukon is not inside the GST figure.

The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box left off. Title, registration, and a documentation fee are not inside the tax. A luxury tax imposed by another statute is not added.

## How the calculation works

Start with the dealer price before tax. The form applies the federal tax and the provincial or territorial vehicle tax and adds them. A trade-in reduces the taxed price only after you say it qualifies. General merchandise on the sales-tax page is not this invoice.

## Private sales and exclusions

The worked lines leave the trade-in off. A private sale does not invent PST. Do not average Yukon GST and British Columbia’s dealer tax.

## Common mistakes

Putting half of British Columbia’s provincial vehicle tax on a Yukon dealer sale. British Columbia’s provincial amount on the sample is {example:BC.vehicle.29400.provincial}. Adding a plate fee to {example:YT.vehicle.29400.tax}. Using the Alberta page and forgetting the registry is Yukon’s.
