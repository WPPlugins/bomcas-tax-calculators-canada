## What this tax is

Dealer tax follows the sales-tax table at {rate:NL.sales.hst}. Labrador is not a separate dealer rate. A private sale is where book value can replace a lower price. These examples are dealer sales.

The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box left off. Title, registration, and a documentation fee are not inside the tax. A luxury tax imposed by another statute is not added.

## How the calculation works

Start with the dealer price before tax. The form applies the federal tax and the provincial or territorial vehicle tax and adds them. A trade-in reduces the taxed price only after you say it qualifies. General merchandise on the sales-tax page is not this invoice.

## Private sales and exclusions

When wholesale is higher and no affidavit is filed, private tax can follow the book value rather than the price. Filing an affidavit is a factual step this screen does not take for you. The dealer lines below do not use book value.

## Common mistakes

Using Nova Scotia’s current rate on a dealer sale here. The current Nova Scotia dealer tax is {example:NS.vehicle.31920.tax}. Treating a dealer sale as a private book-value sale. Adding registration to {example:NL.vehicle.32200.tax}.
