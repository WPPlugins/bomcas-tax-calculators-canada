## What this tax is

A licensed dealer sale uses the dealer price. GST and Manitoba retail sales tax are the tax line when the row says so. A private purchase is not priced like the dealer examples.

The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box left off. Title, registration, and a documentation fee are not inside the tax. A luxury tax imposed by another statute is not added.

## How the calculation works

Start with the dealer price before tax. The form applies the federal tax and the provincial or territorial vehicle tax and adds them. A trade-in reduces the taxed price only after you say it qualifies. General merchandise on the sales-tax page is not this invoice.

## Private sales and exclusions

On a private purchase the base is the wholesale value unless a same-kind trade-in applies or the form’s other published exception is met. Leaving the private path on the dealer examples would be the wrong base. The worked lines stay on the dealer path.

## Common mistakes

Using the general sales-tax page as the car invoice. Copying Saskatchewan’s dealer tax of {example:SK.vehicle.29400.tax} and ignoring Manitoba retail sales tax. Adding registration to the tax line.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Saskatchewan’s tax on the dealer price is {example:SK.vehicle.29400.tax}. Manitoba’s is {example:MB.vehicle.29400.tax}. Ontario’s dealer tax is harmonized and uses a different example key. Do not paste a Manitoba private wholesale rule onto an Ontario private sale. Ontario uses the greater of price and wholesale, and a trade-in does not reduce that private base.
