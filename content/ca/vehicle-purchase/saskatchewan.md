## What this tax is

A dealer sale in Saskatchewan is not the general-merchandise total from the sales-tax page. GST and the provincial vehicle tax are both possible. A private sale is a different base.

The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box left off. Title, registration, and a documentation fee are not inside the tax. A luxury tax imposed by another statute is not added.

## How the calculation works

Start with the dealer price before tax. The form applies the federal tax and the provincial or territorial vehicle tax and adds them. A trade-in reduces the taxed price only after you say it qualifies. General merchandise on the sales-tax page is not this invoice.

## Private sales and exclusions

A low private sale can be exempt when both the price and the book value are at or under the ceiling the bulletin publishes. This estimate does not apply that exemption unless the form’s rule is met. Do not type the ceiling from memory if the file is what the screen uses. A dealer sale does not get that private exemption.

## Common mistakes

Using Alberta’s dealer tax of {example:AB.vehicle.29400.tax}, which has no provincial vehicle tax. Treating a dealer sale as the low-value private exemption. Adding registration to {example:SK.vehicle.29400.tax}.
