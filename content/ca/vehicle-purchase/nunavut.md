## What this tax is

The tax line is GST. There is no territorial vehicle tax in the row and no registration tax inside the figure. A Manitoba retail-sales-tax habit does not belong on this dealer sale.

The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box left off. Title, registration, and a documentation fee are not inside the tax. A luxury tax imposed by another statute is not added.

## How the calculation works

Start with the dealer price before tax. The form applies the federal tax and the provincial or territorial vehicle tax and adds them. A trade-in reduces the taxed price only after you say it qualifies. General merchandise on the sales-tax page is not this invoice.

## Private sales and exclusions

A private sale uses this table’s rule, which does not invent a wholesale PST the territory does not charge. Registry fees are extra. The worked lines are dealer sales without a trade-in.

## Common mistakes

Adding Manitoba’s provincial vehicle tax because a southern bill of sale had a PST box. Manitoba’s dealer tax is {example:MB.vehicle.29400.tax}. Folding registration into {example:NU.vehicle.29400.tax}. Using the Northwest Territories page as if the registries were the same.

## Questions


### What is charged?

GST. On the first dealer price the tax is {example:NU.vehicle.29400.tax}.

### Why not copy a Manitoba bill of sale?

Manitoba charges retail sales tax on a dealer vehicle. Its tax is {example:MB.vehicle.29400.tax}.

### Is registration included?

No.

### Is the Northwest Territories the same registry?

No. The sample GST matches. The registry does not.

### Does a trade-in apply?

Only when you say so. The worked line leaves it off.

### Is this an invoice?

No. It is a {year_label} estimate, checked {updated}.
