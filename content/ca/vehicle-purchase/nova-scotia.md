## What this tax is

Dealer tax follows the sales-tax table. The current harmonized rate is {rate:NS.sales.hst}. Before {date:NS.sales.latest_from} the rate was {rate:NS.sales.prior.hst}. These dealer examples use the current row. A private sale can use a different base. Do not price it with the dealer line.

The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box left off. Title, registration, and a documentation fee are not inside the tax. A luxury tax imposed by another statute is not added.

## How the calculation works

Start with the dealer price before tax. The form applies the federal tax and the provincial or territorial vehicle tax and adds them. A trade-in reduces the taxed price only after you say it qualifies. General merchandise on the sales-tax page is not this invoice.

## Private sales and exclusions

The worked lines leave the trade-in off and they are not a private sale. Registration is extra. A book-value rule from Newfoundland and Labrador is not this province’s dealer invoice.

## Common mistakes

Using the pre-change rate of {rate:NS.sales.prior.hst} on a current dealer sale. Copying New Brunswick’s dealer tax without checking the rate. Adding a separate GST line on top of harmonized tax.
