## What this tax is

Prince Edward Island income tax applies to taxable income in addition to federal tax. The estimate is employment income for a single person. Claims the form does not ask for are omitted. Harmonized sales tax is not added to the income-tax result.

The basic personal amount is {bpa:PE}. The credit rate is {rate:PE.income.credit}. A balance is due {deadline:balance_due}. Filing by {deadline:file_self_employed} does not postpone the balance.

## How the calculation works

Deduct only the enhanced portion of CPP. EI premiums are not deductible. Apply the federal brackets and the Island brackets to the same taxable income. Island tax starts at {rate:PE.income.first} and the top rate is {rate:PE.income.top}. There is no surtax in this row and no abatement of federal tax.

A wage of {example:PE.income.155000.employment} draws combined tax of {example:PE.income.155000.totalTax}. Only the slice above the last threshold is taxed at {rate:PE.income.top}.

## Credits and programs

The basic personal amount is the credit in this estimate. The Prince Edward Island sales-tax credit and low-income credits are not subtracted. The Canada child benefit is not in the total.

## Common mistakes

Using Nova Scotia’s basic amount of {bpa:NS}, or Nova Scotia’s HST change date, on an Island return. Treating {example:PE.income.42000.afterTax} as a paycheque. Adding HST to the income-tax figure.

## Questions

### Is this the same as Nova Scotia?

No. The first rates are {rate:PE.income.first} and {rate:NS.income.first}. The basic amounts are {bpa:PE} and {bpa:NS}.

### Is HST included?

No. HST is charged on taxable supplies. It is not a line on this income-tax estimate.

### Does the top rate cover the whole wage?

No. {rate:PE.income.top} applies to the top slice. The first wage’s combined tax is {example:PE.income.42000.totalTax}.

### Are sales-tax credits included?

No. They are not subtracted from the income tax.

### Can I assess a return from this?

No. It is a {year_label} estimate, checked {updated}.

### Does typing a wage upload it?

No, unless you later ask for an email or a PDF.
