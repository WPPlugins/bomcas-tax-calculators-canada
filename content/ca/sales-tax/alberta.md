## What this tax is

Alberta does not levy a provincial sales tax. On a standard taxable supply the tax is the federal goods and services tax. There is no PST line and no HST. This page is for that general supply. It is not a vehicle invoice, not real property, and not a hotel or tourism levy.

## How the calculation works

The pre-tax price is multiplied by the GST rate in the {year_label} sales table. The provincial, harmonized, and Quebec components in the worked line are zero because those taxes are not charged here. A zero is the result, not a missing rate.

## Worked examples

A pre-tax price of {example:AB.sales.2500.amount} draws GST of {example:AB.sales.2500.gst}. Provincial sales tax is {example:AB.sales.2500.pst}, HST is {example:AB.sales.2500.hst}, and QST is {example:AB.sales.2500.qst}. The tax is {example:AB.sales.2500.tax}, and the amount with tax is {example:AB.sales.2500.total}.

At {example:AB.sales.18000.amount}, the tax is {example:AB.sales.18000.tax} and the amount with tax is {example:AB.sales.18000.total}. The tax moved with the price. Alberta did not add a second provincial layer.

## What is left out

A vehicle can still attract GST, and a dealer sale is on the vehicle page so the provincial rule is not confused with this general supply. Exempt supplies, including many health and educational supplies, are not given a silent tax. A municipal fee is not looked up from a postal code.

## Common mistakes

Adding a neighbour’s PST “just in case.” British Columbia tax on {example:BC.sales.2500.amount} is {example:BC.sales.2500.tax}, which includes PST. Using that figure in Alberta overcharges. Another mistake is typing the tax-included total of {example:AB.sales.2500.total} into the before-tax box.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

British Columbia charges GST and PST. On {example:BC.sales.2500.amount} the tax is {example:BC.sales.2500.tax}, higher than Alberta’s {example:AB.sales.2500.tax} by the PST.

Saskatchewan charges a provincial sales tax as well. Its tax on that price is {example:SK.sales.2500.tax}. The supply is taxed under the rules of the place of supply.

## Questions

### Does Alberta have PST or HST?

No. The worked tax of {example:AB.sales.2500.tax} on the first price is GST. PST and HST on that line are zero.

### Why would I open the British Columbia page?

Only if the supply is taxed in British Columbia. Their tax on the same pre-tax price is {example:BC.sales.2500.tax}.

### Does zero PST mean the form failed?

No. Alberta’s rate row has no provincial sales tax. GST is still calculated.

### Are vehicles included?

No. Use the vehicle page. This page is a general taxable supply.

### When is GST remitted?

On the filing frequency assigned to the GST account. This calculator does not look that date up.

### Is this a return?

No. It is an estimate from the {year_label} table, checked {updated}.
