## What this tax is

Ontario charges harmonized sales tax on a standard taxable supply. The GST and the old provincial sales tax are not collected as two lines. This page is that general supply. It is not a vehicle invoice, not land transfer tax, and not a Toronto municipal fee. Toronto does not add a sales tax on top of the harmonized tax in this table.

## How the calculation works

The price before tax is multiplied by the harmonized rate of {rate:ON.sales.hst}. The GST, PST, and QST components on the worked line are zero because those taxes are not charged separately. The harmonized amount is the tax. Income tax, including Ontario surtax, is not added to a shop receipt.

## Worked examples

A pre-tax price of {example:ON.sales.2500.amount} draws harmonized tax of {example:ON.sales.2500.hst}. The tax line is {example:ON.sales.2500.tax} and the amount with tax is {example:ON.sales.2500.total}. GST on that line is {example:ON.sales.2500.gst}, which is zero because the tax is harmonized.

At {example:ON.sales.18000.amount} the tax is {example:ON.sales.18000.tax}. The tax moved with the price. Nothing on the receipt is Quebec sales tax.

## What is left out

A vehicle can be taxed differently, including on a private sale. Use the vehicle page. Exempt supplies are not given a silent tax. A real-property transaction is not this calculator.

## Common mistakes

Adding a GST line on top of the harmonized tax. Using Quebec’s sales tax because a supplier’s form has a QST box. Typing the tax-included total of {example:ON.sales.2500.total} into the before-tax field. Dating the example with Nova Scotia’s rate-change day.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Quebec charges GST of {rate:QC.sales.gst} and QST of {rate:QC.sales.qst} as separate amounts. On {example:QC.sales.2500.amount} the Quebec tax is {example:QC.sales.2500.tax}, against Ontario’s {example:ON.sales.2500.tax}. They are not the same tax with a different name.

Manitoba charges GST plus retail sales tax. Its tax on that price is {example:MB.sales.2500.tax}. A supply is taxed where the place-of-supply rules put it, not in both provinces.

## Questions

### Is there a separate GST?

No. The worked GST line is {example:ON.sales.2500.gst}. The tax is the harmonized amount of {example:ON.sales.2500.hst}.

### Does Toronto add sales tax?

Not in this table. Municipal land transfer tax is a different tax, on the land-transfer page.

### Why would the Quebec figure differ?

Because Quebec charges QST as well as GST. Ontario charges one harmonized tax at {rate:ON.sales.hst}.

### Are vehicles included?

No. Use the vehicle page. A private sale is not this general supply.

### When is the tax remitted?

On the filing frequency of the account. This calculator does not look that date up.

### Is this a return?

No. It is the {year_label} table, checked {updated}.
