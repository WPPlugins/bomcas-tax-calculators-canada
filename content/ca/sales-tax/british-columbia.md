## What this tax is

A taxable supply in British Columbia is charged goods and services tax and provincial sales tax. The two taxes are separate. They are not harmonized into one HST. This page prices a standard taxable supply. It does not price a vehicle, real property, a hotel levy, or an exempt supply.

The worked prices use the example date in the {year_label} table. Type the amount before tax. If the price you were given already includes tax, switch the form so it removes tax instead of adding it.

## How the calculation works

GST is the federal portion. PST is the British Columbia portion. The form adds each one to the pre-tax amount. A zero on one line means that tax is not charged on this kind of supply. It does not mean the form failed.

## What is not included

Vehicles have their own page because the provincial vehicle rule is not always the general PST rate. Real property is not a taxable supply of this kind. A municipal hotel tax is not looked up. A reseller’s exemption is not assumed. If you are exempt, this estimate is the tax you would have charged without that exemption.

## Common mistakes

Typing {example:BC.sales.2500.total} into the before-tax box charges tax on a price that already includes tax. Adding British Columbia income tax to the receipt double-counts a tax the customer does not pay at the till. Treating PST as HST misstates the return you file: GST and PST are reported to different administrations.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Alberta charges GST and does not charge a provincial sales tax. On {example:AB.sales.2500.amount}, Alberta tax is {example:AB.sales.2500.tax}, against {example:BC.sales.2500.tax} in British Columbia. The difference is the PST.

Yukon also has no territorial sales tax of the PST kind. Its tax on the same price is {example:YT.sales.2500.tax}. Do not average British Columbia and Alberta and call the result a blended rate. The supply is taxed where the rules say it is taxed.

## Questions

### Are GST and PST one tax in British Columbia?

No. GST is federal and PST is provincial. The total on the first example, {example:BC.sales.2500.tax}, is the sum of the two.

### Does this page include tax on a car?

No. A vehicle can use a different provincial base. Use the vehicle page for a dealer sale.

### What if my price already includes tax?

Switch the form to remove tax. Do not type {example:BC.sales.2500.total} into the before-tax field.

### Is a zero PST a broken form?

Only if the supply is actually taxable for PST. On this general page a zero means that component is not in the rate row for the supply being priced.

### When do I remit?

GST remittance follows the frequency on the program account. PST has its own return. This calculator does not look up either cycle.

### Is this a filing?

No. It is an estimate from the {year_label} table, checked {updated}.
