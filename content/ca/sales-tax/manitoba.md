## What this tax is

Manitoba charges the federal goods and services tax and a retail sales tax on a standard taxable supply. The retail tax is not harmonized sales tax. Ontario’s single HST line is the wrong model. This page is a general supply. A vehicle can use the wholesale value on a private sale, which is a different page.

## How the calculation works

GST at {rate:MB.sales.gst} and retail sales tax at {rate:MB.sales.pst} are calculated from the pre-tax price and added. The HST and QST lines are zero. The retail tax is the PST component on the worked line. Income tax is not part of the receipt.

## Worked examples

On {example:MB.sales.2500.amount}, GST is {example:MB.sales.2500.gst} and retail sales tax is {example:MB.sales.2500.pst}. The tax is {example:MB.sales.2500.tax} and the amount with tax is {example:MB.sales.2500.total}.

On {example:MB.sales.18000.amount} the tax is {example:MB.sales.18000.tax}. Both components moved with the price. The retail rate did not jump to a higher band because this supply is not an income-tax bracket.

## What is left out

Some goods are exempt from retail sales tax. The form does not apply an exemption you did not establish. Vehicles, especially private purchases, are on the vehicle page. A municipal tax is not inferred from an address.

## Common mistakes

Charging Saskatchewan’s provincial rate of {rate:SK.sales.pst} on a Manitoba supply. Collapsing the two taxes into Ontario’s harmonized rate of {rate:ON.sales.hst}. Treating a zero HST line as a failure. Entering the tax-included total as the price.

## Recent changes

GST of {rate:MB.sales.gst} and retail sales tax of {rate:MB.sales.pst} are the {year_label} row checked {updated}. Nova Scotia’s date is not used.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Saskatchewan charges provincial sales tax at {rate:SK.sales.pst} plus GST. Its tax on the sample price is {example:SK.sales.2500.tax}, against Manitoba’s {example:MB.sales.2500.tax}.

Ontario charges harmonized tax only. Its tax on that price is {example:ON.sales.2500.tax}. A business that sells into both provinces has to apply the place-of-supply rule, not an average.

## Questions

### Why two lines?

GST and retail sales tax are both charged. On the first price they are {example:MB.sales.2500.gst} and {example:MB.sales.2500.pst}.

### Is this HST?

No. HST on the line is {example:MB.sales.2500.hst}. The provincial piece is retail sales tax at {rate:MB.sales.pst}.

### Is Saskatchewan the same?

No. Saskatchewan’s provincial rate is {rate:SK.sales.pst}. The sample taxes differ.

### Are private vehicle sales here?

No. Bulletin rules for the wholesale value belong on the vehicle page.

### Does zero QST mean a bug?

No. Manitoba does not charge QST.

### Is this a return?

No. It is the {year_label} table, checked {updated}.
