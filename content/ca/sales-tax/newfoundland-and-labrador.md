## What this tax is

Newfoundland and Labrador, including Labrador, charges harmonized sales tax at {rate:NL.sales.hst} on a standard taxable supply. The table does not set a different rate for Labrador. This page is a general supply. Income tax, which has its own high top rate, is not added to the receipt.

## How the calculation works

The pre-tax price is multiplied by {rate:NL.sales.hst}. One harmonized amount is the tax. The GST line is zero because GST is inside the harmonized tax, not beside it. The Nova Scotia change date is not a switch for this province.

## Worked examples

A price of {example:NL.sales.2500.amount} draws tax of {example:NL.sales.2500.tax}. The amount with tax is {example:NL.sales.2500.total}. The harmonized component is {example:NL.sales.2500.hst}.

A price of {example:NL.sales.18000.amount} draws tax of {example:NL.sales.18000.tax}. The rate did not step up because the price was larger. Harmonized tax on a standard supply is proportional.

## What is left out

Rebates for books or other point-of-sale items are not assumed. Vehicles are on the vehicle page, where a private sale can use book value. Exempt supplies stay exempt.

## Common mistakes

Using Nova Scotia’s current rate of {rate:NS.sales.hst} after hearing that “the Atlantic rate changed.” New Brunswick matches this province’s rate. Nova Scotia does not, after {date:NS.sales.latest_from}. Adding income tax to the till.

## Recent changes

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Nova Scotia’s current tax on the sample price is {example:NS.sales.2500.tax}. This province’s tax is {example:NL.sales.2500.tax}. The gap is the rate cut Nova Scotia made and this province did not.

New Brunswick’s tax on that price is {example:NB.sales.2500.tax}. The rates match. The income-tax tables do not, so a person who moves still opens the income-tax page for the province where they are resident.

## Questions

### Is Labrador different?

Not in this sales table. The rate is {rate:NL.sales.hst} for the province.

### Did the Nova Scotia cut apply here?

No. The cut is dated {date:NS.sales.latest_from} and it is Nova Scotia’s row.

### Why is there no GST line?

The tax is harmonized. GST of {example:NL.sales.2500.gst} means it is not charged separately.

### Is a larger price taxed at a higher rate?

Not this standard supply. The second price’s tax is {example:NL.sales.18000.tax}, at the same rate.

### Are vehicles included?

No. Private sales can use a different base. Open the vehicle page.

### Is this a return?

No. It is the {year_label} table, checked {updated}.
