## What this tax is

Saskatchewan charges GST and a provincial sales tax on a standard taxable supply. The provincial rate is {rate:SK.sales.pst}, not Manitoba’s retail rate and not Alberta’s absence of a provincial tax. This page is a general supply. A low-value private vehicle can be exempt under a rule that lives on the vehicle page, not here.

## How the calculation works

GST at {rate:SK.sales.gst} and provincial sales tax at {rate:SK.sales.pst} come off the pre-tax price and are added. HST and QST are zero. The tax line is the sum. Do not harmonize the two amounts into one labelled HST figure.

## Worked examples

On {example:SK.sales.2500.amount}, GST is {example:SK.sales.2500.gst} and provincial sales tax is {example:SK.sales.2500.pst}. The tax is {example:SK.sales.2500.tax}. The amount with tax is {example:SK.sales.2500.total}.

On {example:SK.sales.18000.amount} the tax is {example:SK.sales.18000.tax}. Someone who dropped the provincial component would charge only the GST. That would be an Alberta result, and this supply is not in Alberta.

## What is left out

Exemptions for particular goods are not applied in silence. Vehicles are a separate calculator. Income tax is not on the receipt.

## Common mistakes

Copying Alberta’s tax of {example:AB.sales.2500.tax} because the provinces share a border. Using Manitoba’s rate of {rate:MB.sales.pst}. Calling the provincial tax “HST” and then adding GST a second time.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Alberta charges GST only. On the sample price Alberta’s tax is {example:AB.sales.2500.tax}. Saskatchewan’s is {example:SK.sales.2500.tax}. The difference is the provincial sales tax.

Manitoba’s retail rate is {rate:MB.sales.pst}. Manitoba’s tax on the same price is {example:MB.sales.2500.tax}. Place of supply chooses the province. It does not blend the rates.

## Questions

### What is the provincial piece?

{rate:SK.sales.pst}, shown as {example:SK.sales.2500.pst} on the first price. GST is separate, at {rate:SK.sales.gst}.

### Why not use the Alberta receipt?

Alberta does not levy this provincial tax. Using it undercharges a Saskatchewan supply.

### Is the tax harmonized?

No. HST on the line is {example:SK.sales.2500.hst}.

### Where do vehicle exemptions live?

On the vehicle page. A low private sale is not decided here.

### Can I type a tax-included price?

Only if the form is set to back the tax out. Otherwise type the price before tax, not {example:SK.sales.2500.total}.

### Is this a return?

No. It is the {year_label} table, checked {updated}.
