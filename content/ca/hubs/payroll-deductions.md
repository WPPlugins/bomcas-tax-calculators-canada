## What these pages estimate

Each page estimates one paycheque: federal withholding, provincial or territorial withholding, and the employee pension and employment-insurance amounts. Outside Quebec the pension plan is the Canada Pension Plan, at {rate:CPP.employee} up to {money:CPP.ympe}, and employment insurance is {rate:EI.employee}. In Quebec the pension plan is the Quebec Pension Plan, employment insurance uses the Quebec rate, and the Quebec Parental Insurance Plan is a further amount.

The employee’s net is not the remittance. The remittance includes the employer’s shares and follows the remitter type, which this index does not look up.

## How to choose a page

Open the province or territory of the payroll. A head office in Ontario does not make a Manitoba payroll an Ontario cheque. British Columbia’s July withholding rate applies from {deadline:bc_payroll_july} on the British Columbia page only.

The Quebec sample does not produce a net. Do not subtract another province’s net from zero and call that gap the Quebec cheque. Unavailable means the screen did not calculate it. The Quebec page shows the pension, insurance, and federal amounts it does have.

## What the index does not do

It does not decide claim codes, bonuses, or taxable benefits. It does not call provincial tax a municipal wage tax. It does not put harmonized sales tax on a paycheque. Nova Scotia’s sales-tax change is not a payroll change.

## Questions

### Why is the Quebec net missing?

The sample did not calculate Quebec income-tax withholding through to a net. Do not write zero and remit nothing.

### Is the net what I send to the agency?

No. Remit withheld tax and the employer portions on the remitter’s date.

### Does British Columbia’s July rate apply in Alberta?

No. Alberta does not use {rate:BC.payroll_july}.

### Are the territories free of tax because they have no sales tax?

No. Territorial income tax is still withheld. GST-only sales tax is a different page.

### Does this page file a payroll remittance?

No. It estimates the withholding on one pay. The remittance is still your filing.
