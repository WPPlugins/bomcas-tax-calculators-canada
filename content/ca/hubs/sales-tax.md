## What these pages estimate

Each page prices a standard taxable supply. Alberta, the Northwest Territories, Nunavut, and Yukon charge GST only. British Columbia, Manitoba, and Saskatchewan charge GST plus a provincial or retail tax, at different provincial rates. Ontario and the Atlantic provinces charge a single harmonized tax, and Nova Scotia’s rate changed on {date:NS.sales.latest_from}. Quebec charges GST and QST as separate amounts.

The pages do not look up a city rate from a postal code. They do not price a vehicle. They do not charge land transfer tax.

## How to choose a page

Use the place of supply, which is not always the seller’s head office and not always the buyer’s kitchen. A good shipped from British Columbia into Yukon is not automatically British Columbia PST. A template that still prints Nova Scotia’s old rate after {date:NS.sales.latest_from} is wrong for a current Nova Scotia supply and is also wrong if you paste that date onto Ontario.

A zero provincial line in Alberta or the territories is the result. It is not a broken form. A zero GST line in an HST province is also the result, because the tax is on the harmonized line.

## What the index does not do

It does not add two provinces’ tax on one price. It does not file a GST return or tell you your filing frequency. Exempt and zero-rated supplies are not reclassified in silence.

## Questions

### Why do some GST lines show zero?

In a harmonized province the tax is the single harmonized amount. GST is not charged again beside it.

### Why do some provincial lines show zero?

Alberta and the three territories do not levy a provincial or territorial sales tax on a standard supply.

### Does Nova Scotia’s date change the other provinces?

No. {date:NS.sales.latest_from} is the Nova Scotia change. The other rows do not move that day.

### Are vehicles on these pages?

No. Open the vehicle calculator. Private sales use different bases.

### Does the sales-tax page file a return?

No. It estimates the tax on the amount you enter. It does not file a return.
