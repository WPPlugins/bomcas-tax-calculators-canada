## What these pages estimate

Each page prices the tax on a vehicle. The worked lines are dealer sales with the trade-in box off. Registration, title, and a documentation fee are not in the tax. A luxury tax from another statute is not added.

Dealer tax follows the sales-tax system: GST only in Alberta and the territories, GST plus a provincial tax in the PST provinces, harmonized tax in the HST provinces, and GST plus QST in Quebec. Nova Scotia’s dealer examples use the rate in force after {date:NS.sales.latest_from}.

## Private sales are not one rule

Ontario’s private tax uses the greater of price and wholesale, and a trade-in does not reduce that base. Manitoba’s private tax uses the wholesale value unless a same-kind trade-in applies. Prince Edward Island uses the greater of price and wholesale, with a family-transfer box that starts off. New Brunswick uses fair value. Newfoundland and Labrador can use book value when wholesale is higher and no affidavit is filed. Quebec uses the greater of the price and an estimated value, and GST to a non-registrant can be zero. Saskatchewan can exempt a low private sale when the price and the book value are both at or under the published ceiling. Alberta and the territories do not grow a provincial vehicle tax the table does not contain.

Do not carry one province’s private rule across the border.

## What the index does not do

It does not look up a plate fee. It does not average British Columbia’s dealer tax with Yukon’s GST. It does not treat the general sales-tax page as a car invoice.

## Questions

### Why is registration missing?

It was never part of the tax line. Ask the registry for the fee.

### Are the worked prices private sales?

No. They are dealer sales. Switch the form before you rely on a private result.

### Does Nova Scotia’s date change every dealer in the country?

No. It changes Nova Scotia.

### Does a trade-in reduce tax?

Only when you say the trade-in qualifies, and not on the private sales where the statute ignores it. Ontario’s private base is one of those.

### Does this page include dealer fees?

No. It estimates the tax. Registration and dealer fees are separate.
