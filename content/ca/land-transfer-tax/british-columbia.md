## What this tax is

Property transfer tax is paid when a property is registered in British Columbia. This page uses the general table. The first-time home buyer’s exemption is a box you turn on only after you have decided the buyer qualifies. It is not assumed. A foreign-buyer tax is not in these prices. A further tax on a high-value residential property sits outside the general bands and is not in the worked prices below.

## How the calculation works

The price is split across the general bands. Each band has its own rate, and only the slice inside that band is charged at that rate. The tax is due on registration, not on the individual income-tax balance-due date of {deadline:balance_due}.

## Worked prices

A price of {example:BC.land.650000.price}, with no first-time claim, shows transfer tax of {example:BC.land.650000.tax}. That figure is the general table only.

The second price is {example:BC.land.920000.price}, and the tax is {example:BC.land.920000.tax}. The extra tax on the higher price is the marginal band, not a second copy of the rate that applied to the cheaper purchase.

## Exemptions and programs

The first-time exemption can reduce or remove the tax when the buyer and the property qualify. Newly built homes have had their own exemption conditions. This form does not decide eligibility. Turning the box on without the conditions being met produces a number you cannot use. Leave it off, as the worked prices do, when you are only reading the general table.

## Common mistakes

Quoting {example:BC.land.650000.tax} as the tax on every purchase. Treating the first-time exemption as automatic. Mixing this tax with income tax on a wage, or with PST on a chattel. Forgetting that a residential value above the further-tax threshold can owe an additional tax this general-table result does not include.

## Recent changes

The general table and the exemption amounts are the {year_label} figures checked {updated}. Exemption thresholds have changed in past budgets. If the file’s check date is before a new threshold, do not invent the new amount.

## Neighbouring jurisdictions

Ontario charges land transfer tax on its own table. On {example:ON.land.650000.price}, Ontario tax is {example:ON.land.650000.tax}, compared with {example:BC.land.650000.tax} in British Columbia. Toronto can add a municipal tax that Ontario’s provincial figure, and British Columbia’s figure, do not include.

Alberta does not levy a provincial land transfer tax of this kind. Do not drop the British Columbia result into an Alberta closing.

## Questions

### Is the first-time exemption already in the worked tax?

No. {example:BC.land.650000.tax} is the general table with the exemption box off.

### When is the tax due?

On registration, not on {deadline:balance_due}.

### Does this include the further tax on an expensive home?

No. That further tax is outside the general bands used for these prices.

### How does Ontario compare on the same price?

Ontario’s tax on {example:ON.land.650000.price} is {example:ON.land.650000.tax}. The tables are not interchangeable, and Toronto may add more.

### Is this legal advice on whether I qualify?

No. It is an estimate of the general table for {year_label}, checked {updated}. Qualification for an exemption is a separate decision.
